# The Grand Escape – Luxury Hotel & Resort

A responsive, modern luxury hospitality web application for **The Grand Escape – Luxury Hotel & Resort**, crafted with a warm palette of white, beige, gold, dark brown, and cream, paired with serif/sans-serif editorial typography, interactive filtering, a live stay-rate calculator, and a reservation booking flow.

**Live Demo:** [https://sanjanachowdary-0410.github.io/The-Grand-Escape-Luxury-Hotel-Resort/](https://sanjanachowdary-0410.github.io/The-Grand-Escape-Luxury-Hotel-Resort/)

---

## Features & Sections

1. **Navigation Bar**
   - Fixed navigation header with brand crest, smooth section scrolling, active state styling, and a responsive mobile drawer menu.
   - Direct links to **Home**, **Rooms**, **Facilities**, **Gallery**, **Pricing**, **Reviews**, **About**, **Contact**, and **Book Now**.

2. **Home / Hero Section**
   - Full-bleed luxury resort backdrop with headline (*"Timeless Elegance at The Grand Escape"*), tagline (*"Where Warm Hospitality Meets Unrivaled Coastal & Garden Serenity"*), and resort overview.
   - **Explore Rooms** and **Book Now** call-to-action buttons plus key property highlights (`120+` bespoke rooms & royal suites, `4.9 ★` traveler rating, `24/7` personal butler & concierge).

3. **Room Categories**
   - Six room and suite tiers with photography, guest capacity, bed configuration, square footage, nightly rates, **View Details** modal previews, and **Book Now** pre-selection:
     - **Standard Room** (`$180 / night` • 2 Guests • Queen Bed • 340 sq ft)
     - **Deluxe Room** (`$260 / night` • 2–3 Guests • King Bed • 450 sq ft)
     - **Executive Room** (`$340 / night` • 3 Guests • King Bed • 540 sq ft)
     - **Premium Suite** (`$490 / night` • 4 Guests • Cal King • 780 sq ft)
     - **Family Room** (`$420 / night` • 5 Guests • 1 King + 2 Twin • 720 sq ft)
     - **Presidential Suite** (`$950 / night` • 6 Guests • 2 Master Kings • 1,850 sq ft)

4. **Hotel Facilities**
   - Ten signature resort amenities with icons and descriptions: **Free Wi-Fi**, **Swimming Pool**, **Restaurant**, **Room Service**, **Spa and Wellness**, **Fitness Center**, **Free Parking**, **Conference Hall**, **Airport Pickup**, and **24/7 Reception**.

5. **Interactive Room & Resort Gallery**
   - Filterable photo showcase across **Bedroom**, **Bathroom**, **Swimming Pool**, **Restaurant**, and **Hotel Exterior** categories with hover zoom effects.

6. **Pricing & Seasonal Packages**
   - Promotional packages with seasonal discounts and perks (**Weekend Serenity Escape** – *Save 20%*, **Royal Romance & Spa Retreat** – *Most Popular*, and **Grand Family Staycation** – *Kids Stay Free*) alongside a side-by-side room rate comparison table.

7. **Customer Reviews**
   - Guest testimonials featuring avatars, names, 5-star ratings, written feedback, and the resort's overall guest score.

8. **Interactive Booking Section**
   - Reservation form collecting **Full Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, **Check-in & Check-out Dates**, **Adults & Children Count**, **Room Category**, **Number of Rooms**, and **Special Requests**.
   - Includes real-time stay duration & estimated total calculation, input validation, and an instant reservation confirmation summary.

9. **About Us**
   - Covers the resort's heritage, founding story, mission and vision, and key reasons travelers choose **The Grand Escape**.

10. **Contact Information & Footer**
    - Displays resort address, phone number, email, check-in/check-out policies, social media links, an embedded Google Map, and a multi-column footer with quick links and copyright details.

---

## Built With

- **HTML5** — Semantic layout structure, accessible modals, and form controls
- **CSS3** — CSS custom properties (variables), CSS Grid & Flexbox, responsive breakpoints, hover animations, and scroll-reveal transitions
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)** — Mobile menu navigation, room detail modal, gallery category filtering, live stay price estimator, form validation, and booking confirmation
- **Google Fonts & Font Awesome** — Luxury editorial typography and UI iconography
