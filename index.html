Our Heritage

A Legacy of Warmth & Refined Luxury

Founded in 1998 as a private coastal estate, The Grand Escape – Luxury Hotel & Resort has evolved into an internationally celebrated sanctuary where classic architecture meets contemporary comfort.

Every detail—from our locally quarried travertine courtyards to our bespoke aromatherapy rituals—is crafted to slow down time and restore the spirit.

Our Mission

To deliver deeply personalized, heartfelt hospitality that transforms every stay into a cherished lifelong memory.

Our Vision

To set the global benchmark for sustainable luxury resorts where elegance, wellness, and nature coexist in harmony.
